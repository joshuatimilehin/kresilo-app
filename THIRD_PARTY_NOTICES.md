# Third-party notices

The layered workspace adds six original SVG reference studies and three graphic maker monograms authored for this prototype. Their creators are explicitly fictional samples. These SVGs contain no imported third-party artwork; existing OpenDesign attribution below remains unchanged.

Orbit cuts, Type rush and Signal shuffle are original HyperFrames renders of those studies using the repository's licensed fonts. Lina Guerrero's Spring Show campaign informed the requested pace; its footage, artwork and fonts are not copied into these loops.

Kresilo retains its React/Nitro application and the existing Octo storage workspace. OpenDesign is an inspected source reference, rather than a second installed application.

The HyperFrames package and CLI are distributed under Apache 2.0 by HeyGen. Kresilo pins hyperframes 0.8.1 and uses its trusted composition renderer. The CLI resolution pattern in server/creative/motion.ts follows nexu-io/open-design apps/daemon/src/media/hyperframes-runtime.ts at 8d820a698908dc279ef3d5aeb4ce6d156211a499 (Apache 2.0); the surrounding Kresilo composition, ownership checks and storage integration are newly written. The OpenDesign license is preserved in third-party/open-design-license.txt. No OpenDesign SQLite database, app backend, community data or provider credentials are copied.

Inter, Instrument Serif and Bodoni Moda are distributed under the SIL Open Font License. Their italic fonts and licenses are preserved in public/assets/kresilo. Phosphor React icons use the MIT license. GSAP, Google Auth Library and the FFmpeg/FFprobe installer packages retain their upstream package notices; the FFmpeg/FFprobe binaries have their own bundled licensing terms. No third-party ownership is asserted over generated Kresilo sample images.

## Open Design

Kresilo's design styles (`src/creative/openDesign/styles.json`) are adapted from the design systems in Open Design (`nexu-io/open-design`, revision `8d820a6`), licensed under the Apache License 2.0. Only generic styles are included; none named after a company, product or person. Each style's rules were shortened, and names of real publications were removed.

The Kami style is based on tw93/kami (MIT, © Tw93 and contributors).
