The problem

Creative work moves forward. The reasoning gets left behind.

Creators have become the human API, carrying context between tools by hand.
Inspiration loses its contextA saved reference forgets where it came from and why it mattered.
Work crosses many toolsEvery handoff drops the intent behind the decision.
The reasoning never travelsFiles move forward. The why stays behind in someone's head.
First hand: Joshua ran a crypto agency and watched this happen in every project
The idea

Culture feeds creativity. Creativity produces culture.

Kresilo connects them.
A social network and creative studio, built as one continuous world: discovery, thought, making, memory and cultural exchange.

Market strategy

Start focused. Grow through what people make and share.

Seed the communityDesigners, illustrators, photographers and other visual thinkers who actively share work, references and process.
Make creation the hookUseful to one creator alone: save a reference, start a project, keep the reasoning. The network adds to that, but it isn't needed on day one.
Grow through contributionPublished work brings new people in. Attribution, remixing and project contributions give creators reasons to return and invite others.
