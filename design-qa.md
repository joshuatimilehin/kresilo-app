## Required fidelity surfaces

**Fonts and typography.** Times/Georgia and Instrument Serif establish the editorial display hierarchy; local Bodoni Moda italic matches the poster's high-contrast character; Inter handles small UI text and kinetic type. Headings wrap deliberately, inputs remain readable and text controls are keyboard reachable. Font weight and exact poster letterforms vary slightly from the generated reference; this is P3 optical refinement, not hidden or truncated content.

**Spacing and layout rhythm.** The collage, culture split, three-column gallery/board and focused editor retain generous whitespace. Large regions, pane positions and mobile fold placement were corrected. No horizontal page overflow was observed at 320, 390, 768 or 1487 pixels. Long histories scroll inside the mobile dialog; their restore controls remain reachable. Native project/audience selects replace illustrative custom menus to keep keyboard and mobile interaction dependable.

**Colors and tokens.** Warm paper #f5f3ee, ink #111311, chartreuse #c7fe02 and muted gray-green establish the approved palette. Dark creator and story pages retain strong artwork contrast. Generated raster lighting creates small variations in the horizon and material colors; no code-drawn replacement artwork or synthetic gradient stands in for an image.

**Image quality and assets.** Original generated raster artwork and consistent fictional portraits replace every required visual asset. The wide Create horizon, paper collage, material images and process desk were inspected at full size before optimization. Fonts and relevant notices are preserved. Phosphor supplies standard icons. Gallery loops use original Kresilo images, not Lina footage. All three sample practices are labelled fictional; no fake active collaborators or real creator attribution is invented.

**Copy and content.** Labels explain the next action and keep source/reason/credit visible. Guest duration and cookie limitations are explicit. Google is visibly unavailable, matching the owner's presentation choice. Proposals state that nothing changes until approval; private previews explain link access and redact board/private context. Sample location describes the maker and does not assert where an imagined photograph was taken.
